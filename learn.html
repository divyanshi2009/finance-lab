01 • REVENUE VS PROFIT

Revenue is not profit.

Revenue is the money a business earns from selling its products or services. Profit is what remains after expenses are deducted.

Revenue = ₹1,00,000
Expenses = ₹70,000
Profit = ₹30,000

02 • PROFIT MARGIN

How much of your revenue becomes profit?

Profit margin shows profit as a percentage of revenue.

Profit Margin = Profit ÷ Revenue × 100

03 • ASSETS & LIABILITIES

What you own vs what you owe.

Assets are resources with value, such as cash, equipment or property. Liabilities are amounts that a business or person owes.

04 • COMPOUND GROWTH

Growth can build on previous growth.

With compounding, returns can themselves earn returns over time. This is why time can have a major effect on long-term growth.

05 • BREAK-EVEN

The point where revenue covers costs.

At the break-even point, total revenue equals total costs. Beyond that point, additional contribution can move toward profit.

06 • GST BASICS

A tax added to eligible goods and services.

GST is an indirect tax system. The actual rate depends on the relevant good or service.
